Midstream Oil & Gas Brazil

Transport, Storage & Processing — Pipeline operations, terminals, gas processing, and logistics coordination across Brazil's energy infrastructure.

Industry Overview

Brazil's midstream sector connects upstream production to downstream distribution through a network of over 9,100 km of oil pipelines, 9,400 km of gas pipelines, and 35+ storage and regasification terminals. Operated primarily by Transpetro (a Petrobras subsidiary), the network handles crude oil transport from offshore platforms, natural gas processing, NGL fractionation, and LNG imports. Growing demand and new production frontiers are driving significant infrastructure expansion and modernization investments.
