Brazil's Oil & Gas Landscape

Brazil ranks among the world's top 10 oil producers, with a complex value chain spanning deep-water exploration in the Pre-Salt layer, an extensive pipeline and terminal network, and one of Latin America's largest fuel distribution markets. The industry faces accelerating digital transformation demands — from reservoir optimization to last-mile delivery — creating opportunities for unified platforms that connect the full value chain.
