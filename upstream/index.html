Upstream Oil & Gas Brazil

Exploration & Production — Deep-water operations, Pre-Salt reserves, and digital transformation in Brazil's E&P sector.

Industry Overview

Brazil's upstream sector is dominated by deep-water and ultra-deep-water operations in the Pre-Salt layer, discovered in 2006. With proven reserves exceeding 15 billion barrels and production surpassing 3.7 million barrels per day, Brazil is the largest oil producer in Latin America. The sector attracts over $40 billion in annual investment, driven by production sharing agreements and an expanding roster of international operators alongside Petrobras.
