# Strategic Shifts & Digital Transformation — Oil & Gas Brazil

## Four Strategic Shifts Reshaping Downstream

The market is moving from volume management to decision orchestration. These four forces compound — and the winners are those who detect, decide, and act fastest.

### Shift 1: Fuel Mix Divergence

Each product now requires its own elasticity model, inventory strategy, and margin playbook.

| Product | Margin Profile | Key Dynamic |
|---------|---------------|-------------|
| Gasoline C | R$0.08-0.12/L | Ethanol parity (70% rule) makes demand elastic daily |
| Diesel S10/S500 | R$0.10-0.18/L | Inelastic demand. Margin via B2B contracts + logistics SLA |
| Hydrated Ethanol | R$0.05-0.10/L | Seasonal (inter-safra = scarcity). Price correlated to sugar |
| Biodiesel | Mandated blend | 15% mandatory. Margin at producer level |
| Aviation (QAV) | R$0.15-0.25/L | Highest margin. Airport logistics = barrier |

**CRM Implication:** Pricing, forecasting, and margin governance must be product-specific. Need product × region × customer segment × contract type matrices.

### Shift 2: Biofuel Acceleration

Regulatory mandates + consumer behavior + carbon markets are accelerating biofuel share.

| Driver | Impact | Timeline |
|--------|--------|----------|
| Ethanol mandate increase | 27% → 30% in gasoline (Fuel of the Future Law) | 2025-2027 |
| Biodiesel B20 | 15% → 20% in diesel. +3B liters demand/year | By 2030 |
| RenovaBio (CBIOs) | Carbon credits mandatory. R$80-120/CBIO | Active, growing |
| SAF (Sustainable Aviation Fuel) | Mandate expected. Brazil positioned as global leader | 2027-2030 |
| Green Diesel (HVO) | Drop-in replacement. Premium pricing | 2026+ |

**CRM Implication:** Customer segmentation must connect fuel preference, sustainability positioning, and CBIO compliance. Data Cloud unifies consumption with carbon credit obligations.

### Shift 3: Channel Fragmentation

One distributor now serves radically different customer archetypes through distinct commercial motions.

| Channel | Buyer Profile | Cycle | Key Motion |
|---------|--------------|-------|-----------|
| Branded Retail | Dealer/franchisee | 3-5yr | Relationship + churn prevention |
| White-Flag Conversion | Independent owner | Transactional → contract | Outbound prospecting |
| B2B Industrial | Mining, thermoelectric | 12-36mo indexed | Technical + logistics + credit |
| Agribusiness | Farms, cooperatives | Seasonal | Seasonal pre-sell + storage |
| Fleet / Transport | Trucking companies | Monthly volume | Card management + SLA |
| TRR (Resellers) | Small distributors | Spot | Price + availability |
| Government | Military, agencies | Auction-based | Compliance-heavy |

**CRM Implication:** Sales Cloud must support 7+ distinct selling motions simultaneously — different pipelines, approvals, pricing, and metrics per segment.

### Shift 4: Logistics as Commercial Weapon

In a commoditized market, delivery reliability becomes THE differentiator.

| Dimension | Competitive Impact | Data Signal |
|-----------|-------------------|------------|
| Delivery SLA | Stockout loses customers permanently | Tank telemetry + consumption |
| Route Density | More stations per route = lower cost per liter | Field Service + geo |
| Shortage Management | Who gets fuel first determines who keeps customers | Priority scoring |
| Compartmented Fleet | Multi-product trucks need sequencing intelligence | Order optimization |
| Seasonal Surge | Harvest spikes diesel +20% in agri states | Historical + crop calendar |

**CRM Implication:** Field Service + Data Cloud + Agentforce transform logistics from reactive dispatch to predictive commercial action. A delivery is a customer experience moment.

### The Convergence

These four shifts compound. Example: A fleet customer (Channel Fragmentation) buying biodiesel-blended diesel (Fuel Mix) needs proactive delivery before harvest (Logistics as Weapon) with CBIO-compliant tracking (Biofuel Acceleration). Only a platform unifying all four dimensions captures the decision advantage.
