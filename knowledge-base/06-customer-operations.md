# Customer Operations — Downstream Oil & Gas Brazil

## Why Customer Operations Matters

Fuel is a commodity — the molecule is identical regardless of brand. Customer operations transforms fuel distribution into a relationship business by creating switching costs, recurring revenue streams, and data advantages that compound over time.

The most profitable distributors aren't those with the lowest fuel cost — they're those with the stickiest customers.

## The Defensive Moat — Four Pillars

### 1. Credit & Financial Services
- In a 14% base rate (Selic) economy, credit = volume lock-in
- Dealers who receive credit from distributor X won't switch to Y
- Working capital for dealers, flexible payment for B2B — creates financial dependency
- **Scale:** R$5-10B in credit exposure across top 3. Bad debt ratio: 1-3% = R$50-300M annual risk
- **Impact:** Credit drives 70%+ of dealer loyalty

### 2. Loyalty Programs
- 40M+ enrolled consumers create recurring traffic + behavioral data goldmine
- Programs evolving from points-only to ecosystems (marketplace, banking, partners)
- Data monetization is next frontier
- **Scale:** Premmia (40M), Shell Box (30M), Km de Vantagens (35M)
- **Impact:** Members visit 2.3x more frequently, spend 15% more per visit

### 3. Fleet Management
- Transport companies integrate fuel cards into operational systems (TMS, payroll, compliance)
- High switching cost — changing provider means re-integrating everything
- **Scale:** ~2M fleet vehicles managed. Average contract: 3-5 years
- **Revenue:** R$500K-50M/year per fleet customer depending on size

### 4. TRR (Last-Mile) Operations
- ~5,000 small distributors buy wholesale and serve fragmented middle-market
- They're both customers AND competitors — managing this duality is critical
- **Scale:** 15% of total diesel volume flows through TRRs
- **Impact:** Losing a TRR = losing access to entire micro-region

**The Existential Question:** If fuel is identical regardless of brand, why would a customer stay? The answer is always customer operations: credit, loyalty, fleet tools, and service.

## Credit Value Chain

1. **Risk Assessment:** Financial analysis, credit scoring, collateral evaluation
2. **Limit Setting:** Dynamic limits based on volume, history, market conditions
3. **Disbursement:** Working capital lines, product financing, investment packages
4. **Monitoring:** Real-time exposure tracking, early warning signals
5. **Collection:** Automated dunning, renegotiation, legal escalation

### Brazil-Specific Credit Complexity
- **Dealer credit:** R$1-5M per station tied to exclusivity contracts
- **Seasonal barter:** Grain-for-fuel operations with crop cycle timing
- **Consigned inventory:** Product on dealer premises, distributor's asset until sold
- **Receivables anticipation:** Factoring dealer card receivables at preferential rates

## Fleet Management — What It Includes

- **Fuel cards:** Per-driver controls, real-time authorization
- **Toll integration:** Single card for fuel + tolls
- **Maintenance:** Preventive scheduling tied to mileage
- **Analytics:** Consumption per vehicle, route efficiency
- **Compliance:** ANTT regulatory, environmental reporting

### Why Fleet Creates Lock-In
- Integrated into TMS, payroll, cost allocation
- Driver rules configured per company (limits per day/transaction/product)
- Migration = reconfiguring 100s-1000s of drivers
- Volume contracts guarantee minimum monthly purchases
