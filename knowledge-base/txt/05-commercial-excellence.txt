# Commercial Excellence — Downstream Oil & Gas Brazil

## Why Commercial Excellence Matters

In a commodity market where everyone sells the same molecule, commercial execution is the only sustainable differentiator. The distributor that detects opportunity faster, closes deals quicker, and retains accounts longer wins — regardless of price.

### The Business Reality

| Challenge | Business Impact | Scale |
|-----------|----------------|-------|
| Dealer Churn | Every station that defects = permanent volume loss. Re-acquisition costs 3-5x retention | 8-12% annual churn = 3,500-5,000 stations switching yearly. ~R$700M/year lost margin |
| B2B Cycle Complexity | Large accounts require 60-90 day sales cycles with multi-stakeholder alignment | Top 100 B2B accounts = 40% of non-retail volume. Losing one = R$10-50M/year |
| Seasonal Demand Spikes | Agribusiness volumes spike 200-300% during harvest | Agro fuel market = ~R$40B/year. First-mover = 6-month volume lock-in |
| Territory Management at Scale | One rep manages 80-150 stations without data-driven prioritization | Dealers visited >2x/month have 60% lower churn |
| White-Flag Conversion | 42% unbranded — largest growth opportunity. Conversion = R$300-800K investment | Payback: 18-36 months. Wrong bet = sunk capital |

**The Core Tension:** Commercial teams must simultaneously prevent churn (defensive), grow existing accounts (farming), and acquire new stations/B2B (hunting) — with the same headcount.

## Two Commercial Battlefields

### Retail (B2C2B) — The Branded Network
- 44,000+ stations — continental scale management
- 49% white-flag — constant conversion battle
- Complex dealer relationships: financing, training, brand compliance
- Consumer loyalty programs competing at the pump
- Convenience retail becoming margin differentiator

### Direct B2B — The Volume Engine
- **Agribusiness:** Seasonal demand, barter contracts, on-farm delivery
- **Mining & Industry:** Long-term contracts, dedicated storage (PAVs)
- **Transport Fleets:** Fleet cards, multi-site delivery
- **Thermal Power:** Massive spot volumes during hydro shortage
- **Government:** Tender processes (licitação), compliance, payment terms

## Retail Network Management — Five Disciplines

1. **Network Planning & Expansion:** Whitespace analysis, competitive mapping, CAPEX prioritization
2. **Dealer Acquisition & Onboarding:** Prospect scoring, contract negotiation, branding/equipment/training
3. **Relationship Management:** Territory management, visit cadence, performance reviews, business plans
4. **Volume & Margin Management:** Volume forecasting, incentive programs, product mix optimization
5. **Retention & Churn Prevention:** Early warning signals, proactive intervention, renewal management

## B2B Sales Machine — Five Disciplines

1. **Market Intelligence & Prospecting:** Sector mapping (harvest calendars, mining CAPEX cycles), account sizing
2. **Complex Sales Execution:** Multi-stakeholder selling, solution design (logistics + credit + pricing)
3. **Account Development:** Share-of-wallet growth, cross-sell (fleet cards, carbon credits, EV charging)
4. **Service Delivery Alignment:** SLA monitoring, dedicated infrastructure (PAVs), proactive issue resolution
5. **Renewal & Retention:** Contract lifecycle, price renegotiation triggers, competitor defense

## Channel Fragmentation — Seven Distinct Selling Motions

| Channel | Buyer Profile | Cycle | Key Motion |
|---------|--------------|-------|-----------|
| Branded Retail | Dealer/franchisee | 3-5yr contract | Relationship + brand investment + churn prevention |
| White-Flag Conversion | Independent owner | Transactional → contract | Outbound prospecting + value proposition |
| B2B Industrial | Mining, thermoelectric | 12-36mo indexed | Technical sale + logistics + credit |
| Agribusiness | Farms, cooperatives | Seasonal (safra-linked) | Seasonal pre-sell + storage logistics |
| Fleet / Transport | Trucking companies | Monthly volume | Card management + route-based supply |
| TRR (Resellers) | Small distributors | Spot / short-term | Price + availability + credit terms |
| Government / Public | Military, agencies | Auction-based | Compliance-heavy. Low margin but guaranteed volume |

**CRM Implication:** Sales Cloud must support 7+ distinct selling motions simultaneously — each with different pipeline stages, approval flows, pricing logic, and success metrics.

## Agentforce Commercial Agents

### Agent 1: Churn Prevention Agent (Retail)
- **Products:** Data Cloud + Einstein AI + Sales Cloud
- **Trigger:** Monitors 44,000 stations daily for early warning composite signals
- **Detection:** Volume decline >10% (3-week rolling) + payment delays + reduced product mix + low engagement
- **Action:** Auto-triggers intervention — alerts territory manager with specific recommended action
- **Value:** Detects churn risk 60 days before unbrand. Each retained station = R$2-5M protected volume

### Agent 2: Opportunity Spotter Agent (B2B)
- **Products:** Data Cloud + Sales Cloud
- **Trigger:** Market signals — harvest forecasts, mining CAPEX, fleet expansion, industrial permits
- **Reasoning:** Correlates market events with account profiles to size demand potential
- **Action:** Creates qualified leads: "Agribusiness X expanding 20% in MT — estimated 5M liters/year diesel"
- **Value:** Reps spend zero time on prospecting research — 100% on relationship and closing

### Agent 3: Deal Desk Agent
- **Products:** Revenue Cloud + Flow
- **Trigger:** B2B proposal submitted for approval in Revenue Cloud pipeline
- **Reasoning:** Reviews margin rules, credit limits, logistics feasibility, strategic value
- **Action:** Auto-approves standard deals (80% volume). Escalates exceptions with full analysis
- **Value:** Deal cycle from 10-15 days to 2-3 days. Standard deals close same-day
